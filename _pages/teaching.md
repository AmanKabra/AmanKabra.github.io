My teaching follows one principle: **internalizing principles for principled externalizing**. Students first confront complex problems without frameworks, discover the limits of intuition, then learn established models to navigate the core tension driving the problem. Out-of-class assignments test whether they can apply those principles to new, ambiguous challenges.

In 2024, I received the **Ossian R. MacKenzie Teaching Award**, given annually to one doctoral student across all departments in the Smeal College of Business for teaching performance. At Penn State, I taught Entrepreneurial Mindset. At Oklahoma State, I will teach organizational behavior.
